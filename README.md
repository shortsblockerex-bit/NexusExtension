NexusExtension
 Nexus helps you take your attention back from the web. Block the sites that eat your day, hide YouTube Shorts, and see only the creators you actually want to watch. Everything is stored on your device. There are no accounts, no analytics and no ads.
▎
▎ BLOCKING THAT WORKS
▎ - Block a site in one click from the toolbar popup, with an optional daily time limit.
▎ - Schedules, a short delay before you can open a blocked site, and a Panic lock that stays on until its timer ends.
▎ - Pause Nexus any time to compare your browsing with and without it.
▎ - Safety copies: before anything big changes, Nexus keeps a copy of your rules and settings, and you can restore it in one click.
▎
▎ YOUTUBE, THE WAY YOU WANT IT
▎ - Hide Shorts, comments, suggested videos, the home feed, end screens and autoplay, each with its own switch.
▎ - Playback speed up to 4x and a volume boost, remembered per site.
▎ - Optional dislike counts (turned on by you; see Privacy).
▎ - A command terminal beside the Create button in YouTube's top bar (also Ctrl+K): type "shorts off", "speed 1.5", "pause 15" and it happens.
▎
▎ INSIGHTS AND WRAPPED
▎ - See your time online, what was blocked, your streak and your top sites, with clear charts.
▎ - Look up any site ("stats youtube.com") for a clean intel page: time spent, rules, and what Nexus does there.
▎ - After six months of use, Nexus tells your story like a music recap: top creators, hours, Shorts versus videos, when you watch, and your longest streak.
▎
▎ FOCUS
▎ - Focus sessions with work and break timers, and calm background sounds (rain, ocean, wind, fireplace) made on your computer.
▎
▎ NEXUS PRO (optional)
▎ - Circle: see only the YouTube channels and Instagram people you choose, and nothing else on the page.
▎ - Blur any part of any page, advanced hide controls, higher speed and boost limits.
▎ - $5 a month or $40 a year, paid on PayPal. Free forever: blocking, Focus, the Shorts switch, the terminal and Insights.
▎
▎ PRIVATE BY DESIGN
▎ Your rules, settings, time statistics and Circle list live in your browser's local storage and never leave it. Nexus asks for access to a site only when you turn on a feature that needs it, and you can take it back in Chrome at any time
